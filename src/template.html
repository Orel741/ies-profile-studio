IES file text

Load the .ies into your renderer or 3D app as the light's IES profile. Unpack the .zip first, or rename the .txt to end in .ies. Straight down (0°) is the light's forward axis. Front, Left, Back and Right are written as C0, C90, C180 and C270; some apps read only one of them. Use the Orientation test preset to check which way Front faces in yours.

Layers

Layers stack bottom to top and combine into the exported light. The curve editor and the preview handles shape the selected layer.

    Add: like a second bulb. Lighten: the brighter wins. Replace: paints over. Mask: a stencil for the layers below. Subtract: removes light.

    File settings

    Candela values are written with a multiplier of 1. Use Absolute (−1) if your importer rescales by the lumen figure. With noise, rays, a turn or an imported file the file uses at most 2.5° horizontal steps; pick 1° for the finest detail.

    3D view

    Drag to orbit (also below the floor or above the ceiling, where they drop out), scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset. Uses the floor height, wall distance and side, and the preview look. A ceiling appears for upward light.
